MARION COUNTY — Apparently, nobody told the Candle Sticks that the candle was supposed to go out.

The championship game was tied 2–2 in the bottom of the sixth when things got chaotic.

Abbi took a thrown ball to the back of the head while heading into third. Somehow, she stayed in the game, stayed on third and eventually crossed the plate to give the Candle Sticks a 3–2 lead.

Then came the seventh.

Pitch Slappers answered with two home runs and suddenly the Candle Sticks were down 3–5 with three outs left in their season.

Then the candle started to flicker.

Phil launched a solo home run.

4–5.

Nick came up and ripped a liner to keep the season alive.

One runner on.

Two outs.

Cal stepped to the plate.

Rain pouring.

Championship on the line.

The count reached 2–2.

One strike from the season being over.

Cal got his pitch.

GONE.

A two-run walk-off home run.

Ballgame.

Championship.

CANDLE STICKS 6. PITCH SLAPPERS 5.

Players stormed the field as the rain continued coming down.

After a season filled with big hits, ridiculous catches, injuries, laughs and teammates showing up for one another, the Marion County Candle Sticks had their first championship.

The weather tried.

Pitch Slappers tried.

But you can't put out a waterproof candle.
